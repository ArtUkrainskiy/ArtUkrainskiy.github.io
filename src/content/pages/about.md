I'm Artem Ukrainskiy. I work on performance in [php-src](https://github.com/php/php-src): string functions, `json_encode()`, `htmlspecialchars()`, PDO, the engine.

These are my public engineering notes. Each post is built around measurements: what was measured, on what, how, and what the numbers say, including the cases where an idea did not work.

The benchmark tool behind most of them is [php-src-bench](https://github.com/ArtUkrainskiy/php-src-bench).
